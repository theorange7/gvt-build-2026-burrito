The problem

You ship. Nobody notices.

Performance reviews reward whoever writes the best self-assessment. The best work — the late-night fix, the cross-team unblock, the mentorship — doesn't make it into the doc.

  • Git has no narrative layer
  • Jira tickets don't capture impact
  • You can't remember Q1 in December
  • Copy-pasting PRs into a Google Doc is not a system
How it works

Three steps.

01
Collect your signal

Connect GitLab, import a commit log, or type notes manually. Everything is encrypted on your device the moment it lands.

02
Pick a mode

Snapshot for any date range. Year-End for the full calendar year. Choose the AI model. Hit generate.

03
Get your wrap

60 seconds later: 10 slides, each targeting a different dimension of your work. Ready to present or share.

What you get

10 slides that tell the story.

Each slide reads only the contributions relevant to it. A slide with no signal is simply skipped — no padding, no filler.
