Work log — Q1/Q2 2024

---

2024-02-14
Shipped the real-time dashboard redesign (PR #418). Replaced the polling-based
feed with a WebSocket connection, cutting perceived latency from ~3s to under
200ms. Three other teams have since adopted the same pattern. Coordinated
rollout across web, iOS, and Android to avoid version skew.

---

2024-03-05
Led a two-hour onboarding session for four new engineers joining the platform
team. Walked through the service architecture, the on-call runbook, and common
failure modes. Wrote up a written version afterwards and added it to the team
wiki so we don't have to repeat the session every quarter.

---

2024-04-22
Identified and fixed a race condition in the job queue (issue #731) that caused
roughly 1-in-500 background tasks to silently drop results. Root cause was an
unguarded read-modify-write on the status field under concurrent workers.
Added an integration test that reproduces the race deterministically.

---

2024-05-30
Drafted and got alignment on a new incident severity matrix for the org. The
previous definition of "SEV-2" had no objective criteria, which led to
inconsistent paging and post-mortem coverage. Ran a review session with five
team leads and published the updated runbook. Two incidents since then have
been classified correctly on first call.
